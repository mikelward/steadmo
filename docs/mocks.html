Steadmo

One place to see glucose, loop state and insulin, and to act on them quickly. The MVP reads CamAPS FX's "My diabetes info" notification and sends you to CamAPS FX when there is something to do. Glooko and takeaway orders come later.

Read this first. No official API exists for CamAPS FX or the Ypsopump. Steadmo can read from them in several ways. It cannot deliver insulin on its own, and it should not try. Every bolus, Boost and Ease-off is confirmed inside CamAPS FX. Steadmo gets you to that screen in one tap, with the amount ready to enter.

MVP architecture

No foreground service is needed: Android keeps the notification listener bound. Readings stay on the phone; the app has no internet permission.

Features, and how each one works

Full-page widget

Jetpack Glance widget sized 4×6 or larger. The service pushes an update on each new reading, so it does not depend on the 30-minute minimum for scheduled widget updates. The graph is drawn to a bitmap, because Glance has no canvas.

Boost and Ease-off

The buttons open CamAPS FX on the right screen, where you set the duration and confirm. The current state and time left come from the notification, or from the Accessibility reader when it is enabled.

Meal presets: 20, 40, 45, Other

Grams of carbs. One row replaces the separate Bolus button: Other opens the meal sheet. Each preset copies the number and opens CamAPS FX's meal entry. As an option, the Accessibility service can type the number in. It never presses confirm. Also exposed as launcher app shortcuts and Quick Settings tiles.

Rate-of-change alerts

  • Low soon: projected below 3.9 within 20 min.
  • Falling fast or rising fast: a double arrow.
  • High soon: a single or double up arrow, above 10.0 within 15 min.
  • The alert's icon is the trend arrow. Each kind repeats at most every 30 min.
  • Later: IOB and carbs context, and an action per alert.

Takeaway pre-bolus prompt (to do)

A notification listener matches "picked up" and "nearby" messages from Deliveroo, Uber Eats and Just Eat. Steadmo suggests your usual takeaway amount (it learns from past meals) and mirrors the prompt to the watch. If you bolus in the next 20 minutes it marks the prompt done.

Watch (not in the MVP)

Phone notifications bridge to Wear OS automatically. A small Wear app adds a tile and a complication fed by the Data Layer API. Actions run on the phone, because CamAPS FX does.

Risks and open questions

Safety

  • Steadmo is a companion display. CamAPS FX and Dexcom alarms stay on and stay authoritative.
  • Never automate the bolus confirmation.
  • Show data age everywhere, and grey a reading out after 10 min.

Fragility

  • Notification text and layouts change with app updates. Keep parsers small, versioned and covered by tests with captured samples.
  • Some OEMs kill background services. Needs battery optimization exemptions.

To verify on your phone

  • Whether "My diabetes info" shows Boost and Ease-off, and how its arrow image maps to ours.
  • Whether CamAPS FX exposes deep links to meal, Boost or Ease-off screens.
  • Deliveroo's current notification wording.
