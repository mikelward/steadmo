Bolusmo

One place to see glucose, loop state and insulin, and to act on them quickly. It reads data from CamAPS FX, Dexcom and Glooko, and sends you to the right app when there is something to do.

Read this first. No official API exists for CamAPS FX or the Ypsopump. Bolusmo can read from them in several ways. It cannot deliver insulin on its own, and it should not try. Every bolus, Boost and Ease-off is confirmed inside CamAPS FX. Bolusmo gets you to that screen in one tap, with the amount ready to enter.

Recommended architecture

A foreground service holds a small local store of readings and events. Adapters feed it, and every screen (widget, app, watch, alerts) reads from it. Nothing leaves the phone unless you opt into Nightscout or Share.

Features, and how each one works

Full-page widget

Jetpack Glance widget sized 4×6 or larger. The service pushes an update on each new reading, so it does not depend on the 30-minute minimum for scheduled widget updates. The graph is drawn to a bitmap, because Glance has no canvas.

Boost and Ease-off

The buttons open CamAPS FX on the right screen, where you set the duration and confirm. The current state and time left come from the notification, or from the Accessibility reader when it is enabled.

Big bolus button and 20/40/45 shortcuts

Carb amounts (this assumes your 20, 40 and 45 are grams). Each one copies the number and opens CamAPS FX's meal entry. As an option, the Accessibility service can type the number in. It never presses confirm. Also exposed as launcher app shortcuts and Quick Settings tiles.

Rate-of-change alerts

  • Slope from a least-squares fit over the last 15 min.
  • Fast rise from 0.1 mmol/L/min, fast fall from −0.1.
  • Projects time to 3.9 and 10.0 and states it.
  • Adds context: IOB, last bolus, carbs logged or not.
  • One action per alert (Ease-off, log carbs, bolus, snooze).

Takeaway pre-bolus prompt

A notification listener matches "picked up" and "nearby" messages from Deliveroo, Uber Eats and Just Eat. Bolusmo suggests your usual takeaway amount (it learns from past meals) and mirrors the prompt to the watch. If you bolus in the next 20 minutes it marks the prompt done.

Watch

Phone notifications bridge to Wear OS automatically. A small Wear app adds a tile and a complication fed by the Data Layer API. Actions run on the phone, because CamAPS FX does.

Risks and open questions

Safety

  • Bolusmo is a companion display. CamAPS FX and Dexcom alarms stay on and stay authoritative.
  • Never automate the bolus confirmation.
  • Show data age everywhere, and grey a reading out after 10 min.

Fragility

  • Notification text and layouts change with app updates. Keep parsers small, versioned and covered by tests with captured samples.
  • Some OEMs kill background services. Needs battery optimisation exemptions.

To verify on your phone

  • Exactly what the CamAPS FX and G7 notifications contain (mode? IOB?).
  • Whether CamAPS FX exposes deep links to meal, Boost or Ease-off screens.
  • Whether CamAPS reads G7 directly or through the Dexcom app on your setup.
  • Deliveroo's current notification wording.

Suggested first milestone

A read-only build: the notification adapters for G7 and CamAPS FX, the Room store, the full-page widget with the graph, and rate-of-change alerts. Buttons open CamAPS FX. This tests whether the data is good enough before any work on Accessibility, Glooko or the watch.
